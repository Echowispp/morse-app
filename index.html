This program uses the common syntax of . being a dot and - being a dash and space between characters, slash between words. For example:

.... . .-.. .-.. --- / .-- --- .-. .-.. -..

= hello world

Compatible letters:

abcdefghijklmnopqrstuvwxyzåäö

Compatible characters:

!?/=:,._'()

Also numbers 0123456789 are compatible
